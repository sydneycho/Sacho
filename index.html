About Us

Our story

SEAW — Sacho Engineering and Automation Works — was founded by Sydney Chongo Chipambala, an Electrical & Electronics Engineer with a background in industrial automation. What began as hands-on engineering and automation support has grown into a full-service technical partner delivering engineering solutions, automation systems, renewable energy, security & networks, and technology solutions to clients across the Copperbelt and beyond.

We bring an engineer's discipline to every project — the same rigor used to troubleshoot substation panels and configure PLCs, applied consistently across consultation, design, supply, installation, commissioning, and maintenance.

Mission

Deliver integrated engineering, automation, and technology solutions that keep our clients' operations efficient, reliable, and safe.

Vision

To be the trusted engineering and automation partner for businesses across Zambia and the wider region.
