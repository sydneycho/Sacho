About Us

Our story

Sacho Innovations was founded in 2022 by Sydney Chongo Chipambala, an Electrical & Electronics Engineer with a background in industrial automation. What began as freelance software work has grown into a studio that builds mobile apps, web platforms, and AI-driven products for clients across six countries.

We bring an engineer's discipline to software delivery — the same rigor used to troubleshoot substation panels and configure PLCs, applied to shipping reliable, well-built digital products.

Mission

Help ambitious teams turn ideas into reliable, well-engineered software.

Vision

To be a trusted technology partner for businesses across Africa and beyond.
